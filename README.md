#  OC Trading Hub

### Trading Technology • AI Automation • Crypto • Forex • Developer Services

**OC Trading Hub** is a trading technology platform designed to bring together crypto trading tools, MetaTrader 5 automation, AI-powered market analysis, trading infrastructure, and custom software development.

##  Security & Risk Management

Security and responsible development are fundamental to the project.

Our development principles include:

* Test strategies with historical data and paper trading before considering live execution.
* Use appropriate position sizing and loss limits.
* Require human approval where configured for real-money trading.
* Protect API credentials and sensitive account information.
* Never publish private keys, wallet seed phrases, passwords, or secret environment files.
* Review dependencies and external integrations.
* Record relevant system activity and errors.
* Avoid promises of guaranteed profits or risk-free trading.

Crypto, forex, and leveraged trading can result in substantial losses, including the loss of all invested capital.
