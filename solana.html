02 / PRE-TRADE REVIEW

A professional research checklist.

01

Contract identity

Confirm the exact address using trusted sources. Do not rely on symbols alone.

02

Liquidity depth

Compare pool liquidity with your intended order size. Thin pools can have severe slippage.

03

Volume quality

Review unusual volume spikes, concentrated activity, and very new pools.

04

Exit and risk plan

Write the invalidation condition and maximum loss before trading elsewhere.
